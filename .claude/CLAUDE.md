# Working on this repository

Read `.claude/FARUQI.md` first. It sets out the Faruqian core, al-tawḥīd as Ismāʿīl Rājī al-Fārūqī expounded it in *Al Tawhid: Its Implications for Thought and Life*, which runs through the themes and content of this site and its eight sister sites. Do not deviate from it.

Then read `.claude/VOICE.md` before writing or editing any article, glossary entry or site page. It sets the voice, the sentence rules and the citation checks the owner asked for on 2026-11-23.

Keep the five base documents to `.claude/skills/theme-docs/SKILL.md` whenever a change affects what they describe.

Every Bible reference links to thebible.exposed, as `.claude/VOICE.md` section 7 sets out (owner's rule of 2026-11-26). Gaps in that site's material go in `.claude/BIBLE-GAPS.md` and are reported to the owner privately, never in published text.
